Welcome to the webpage of the Computational Materials, Mechanics, and Manufacturing Laboratory (CM3 Lab) at the University of Oklahoma (OU)!

Openings

  • Undergraduate students at OU are welcome to contact me for possible research opportunities.
    • Undergraduate students from any major may check the Undergraduate Research Opportunities Program and the First Generation UReCA Fellowship for doing research during the Spring/Fall semesters and the Provost's Summer UReCA Fellowships for doing research during the Summer semesters.
    • Undergraduate students in the Gallogly College of Engineering (GCOE) may check the Engineering Research Entry Apprenticeships and Engineering Research Fellowships for doing research during any semesters.
    • Undergraduate students in the Honors College may check the First-Year Research Experience program, the Honors Research Assistant Program, and the Honors Engineering Research Experience program for doing research during the Spring/Fall semesters.
    • Other undergraduate student research opportunities at OU may be found on the UReCA website.
  • No graduate research assistant openings are currenctly available in our group.
    • Graduate students at OU can check the travel and research grants provided by the graduate college and the graduate student senate. Other scholarships/fellowships for graduate students at OU include CASH, Graduate Student Senate Outstanding Graduate Student Awards, Nancy L. Mergler Dissertation Completion Fellowship, Doctoral Dissertation Prizes, and Graduate Teaching Assistant Awards.
    • Graduate students in GCOE please check the CGOE Graduate Student Travel Award. GCOE additionally offers Dissertation Excellence Award and several recruitment fellowships.
    • Graduate students in AME plase check AME Bulletin Board for scholarship and travel grant opportunities.
  • No postdoc openings are currently available in our group.
  • Self-funded, prospective visiting scholars/students are welcome to contact me.
